Bundled so the labels look right when the app is offline, rather than falling back to
whatever serif the device happens to have.

The file here is the Bold weight, cut down to the characters the app actually uses
(basic Latin and common punctuation). That takes it from 383 KB to 27 KB. If new wording
ever needs characters outside that set, re-cut it from the full font with:

  curl -sS -A "Mozilla/5.0" "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=EB+Garamond:wght@700" 
  # follow the .ttf link in the stylesheet, then:
  python3 -m fontTools.subset EBGaramond-Bold.ttf \
    --unicodes="U+0020-007E,U+00A0,U+00B7,U+2013,U+2014,U+2018,U+2019,U+201C,U+201D,U+2026" \
    --layout-features="kern,liga" --output-file=EBGaramond-Bold.ttf --no-hinting
