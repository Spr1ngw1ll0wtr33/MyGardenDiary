My Garden Diary

Installing it on your phone, and checking everything works

1. Install the diary on your phone

  1. On your phone, open Chrome and go to
    spr1ngw1ll0wtr33.github.io/MyGardenDiary
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. (On some versions it is Add to home screen, then Install.)
  4. Tap Install again to confirm. The grape-vine icon appears on your home screen and in your apps.
  5. From now on, open the diary from its icon, not from Chrome.

Where your entries live: the installed diary shares its storage with Chrome on the phone, so anything you typed into the diary in Chrome on the phone will be there too. What you typed on your PC stays on the PC; the two never mix.

2. Moving files from the phone to your PC

Backups and month-end documents are saved in the phone's Downloads folder. Two ways to move them across, neither of which uses the internet or your cloud account:

By USB cable (nothing to install)

  1. Plug the phone into the PC with its charging cable.
  2. On the phone, pull down from the top of the screen and tap the USB notice, then choose Transferring files (sometimes called File transfer).
  3. On the PC, open Files. The phone appears in the list on the left.
  4. Open Internal storage, then Download, and copy the files into a folder on the PC.

By Zorin Connect (over your home Wi-Fi)

Zorin OS has Zorin Connect built in. With its free phone app installed from the Play Store and the two paired once, you can send a file from the phone's Downloads straight to the PC with Share. Files go directly between the two over your home Wi-Fi, never through the internet. It asks for a number of phone permissions; you only need file sharing, and can say no to the rest.

3. Check every function on the phone

Tick each one as it works. The ticks are remembered on whichever device you tick them on.
